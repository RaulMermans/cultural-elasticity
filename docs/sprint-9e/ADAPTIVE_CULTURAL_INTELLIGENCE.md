# Sprint 9E — Adaptive cultural intelligence

## Scope and evidence status

This change adds `single_hypothesis_v1` inside the existing `AgentRuntime`. It is a bounded way to ask whether available Qloo evidence merits further investigation of one brand and one user-proposed cultural concept. It does not produce a FULL relative analysis, a commercial recommendation, or a measured opportunity score. The production model remains `claude-haiku-5-5`; the typed caller mode is not model-proposed. Production paid admission remains off.

The new decision and outcome tests are **SYNTHETIC scripted regressions**. No new Qloo or Haiku competence run was made for this sprint. The prior [Loewe canary](../sprint-9d/LOEWE_CANARY_REPORT.md) is recorded live **baseline** evidence, not a single-hypothesis agent result: six Qloo calls, no retries, 20 brand tags, 40 returned entities and 40/40 confirmed subtypes. Its Fashion/Model/High Culture/Spanish/Artistic Exploration hypotheses remain broad. The historical Jacquemus agent parent took about 256 seconds, exhausted 32 Qloo calls and produced three PARTIAL results; it is a historical comparison, not a forecast for this route.

`QlooClient`/`QlooTransport` and `qloo-gateway` still own provider execution. `recommend` defaults in the installed harness include intersection for tag signals and union for `include_tags`, so the brand-conditioned request is treated only as a query-scoped observation. A provider status of `needs_input`, `partial`, `degraded`, timeout or error does not become a clean empty result. Retry remains at most once and only for provider-marked retryable errors. Every eligible number remains code-computed from one call and retains its correlation ID.

## Method and conclusion contract

The API accepts a typed single-target `analyze_move` request. A baseline direction or user-proposed direction first passes scan ownership, supporting provenance where applicable, brand identity pinning and admission checks. A user proposal is labelled as user context; it gains no support merely by being selected. The query gate receives the typed mode after interpreting the request, so the model cannot set tool budgets or permissions. The route caps provider dispatches at 12, turns at eight and job duration at four minutes. The frontend shows a preview before an explicit start.

The runtime retains all existing tool/skill permissions, representation validation, evidence collection, claim validator and deterministic finalizer. Reference-set requirements are skipped **only** in this mode. The usual comparative `analyze_move`, `compare_territories` and `explore_frontier` paths keep their existing rules.

`hypothesisAssessment` is a separate versioned projection (`single-hypothesis/v1`) over the canonical result:

- `WORTH_FURTHER_INVESTIGATION`: complete run; concept absent from observed brand profile; a clean, source-linked brand-conditioned query returned distinct members; a validated concept representation exists; and shared profile signals are present. The conditioned members and the shared signals are separate observations, not a claimed entity-level intersection.
- `NOT_SUPPORTED_BY_CURRENT_EVIDENCE`: complete run with source-linked conditioned queries that are all clean and empty, or no resolved concept after only clean empty searches for the exact named target. An additional nonempty or degraded relevant query prevents a negative verdict. This does not assert that the relationship is impossible.
- `INCONCLUSIVE`: the run completed but the positive or clean-empty conditions were not met, including core overlap, weak representation or missing provenance.
- `UNABLE_TO_COMPLETE_RESEARCH`: unresolved brand or incomplete/failed/budget-exhausted run.

The projection carries observed facts, reasons for the outcome, limitations, unknowns, next research step and eligible call IDs. It never invents a score. Qloo does not establish purchase intent, market size, commercial viability or partner feasibility. The existing model summary still passes the claim validator or falls back to a deterministic template.

## Adaptation and usefulness evidence

`backend/tests/agent-single-hypothesis.test.ts` runs the same SYNTHETIC question with and without observed concept members. In the member world the scripted policy validates a representation; in the empty world it declares concept-only before analysis. The runtime supplies distinct member counts and call IDs in the bounded decision context. This proves the context and executor permit evidence-dependent actions; it does **not** prove Haiku independently chose them. The companion assessment file has at least 20 SYNTHETIC counterfactual cases covering duplicate members, source mismatches, missing signals, provider status, clean empties, budget exhaustion and unsupported concepts. Existing comparison and frontier tests provide regression coverage.

For a real recorded example, Loewe's baseline showed verified ready-to-wear and leather-goods category tags as brand core, plus broad cultural hypotheses and named associations; it did **not** validate a boutique-hospitality opportunity. A user may propose that question, but a new agent/Qloo run is needed to assess it. Any UI preview or export before that run remains a question, not a finding.

Human usefulness review: **NOT_EVALUATED**. The review form for two blinded strategists should score, on 0–2 anchored scales, cultural specificity, question relevance, brand-core distinction, evidence grounding, uncertainty calibration, hypothesis-testing behavior and usefulness of the next step; record unsupported claims and seconds/calls to a meaningful answer. Reviewers must identify which Qloo observations changed their decision and whether they would investigate differently from a generic brainstorm. A completed report alone is not a pass. Reviewer identity, case IDs, model and blind assignment must be recorded at review time.

## LLM-only benchmark preparation

`evals/qloo-value/cases.json` remains the frozen seven-case, checksum-pinned condition-B manifest. `pnpm eval:qloo-value` verifies the recorded results and still reports no measured comparison. `pnpm prepare:qloo-value:llm-only` emits a deterministic, hash-pinned request bundle with each exact question, model `claude-haiku-5-5` at high effort, one fixed system prompt, no tools or Qloo/recorded-result access, a fixed output schema (`answer`, `evidence_claims`, `uncertainties`, `next_steps`, empty `citations`) and 1,200 maximum output tokens. It makes **no provider call**. Condition A is **HARNESS_ONLY / LLM_ARM_NOT_RUN**. A future separately approved runner must capture raw request/response, token counts, spend, actual model ID, timestamp and SHA-256 per case. Validate that condition-A citations stay empty; absent source access must not be presented as Qloo evidence. Randomize A/B display to blind reviewers and keep the key separately; use the usefulness rubric above. Errors and omissions remain in the seven-case denominator. No invented A output belongs in the frozen manifest.

## Cost, ownership and operational boundary

The route has code-enforced **call/turn/time ceilings**, not measured latency or cost. Twelve dispatches including retries, eight turns and a four-minute job deadline bound the proposed canary. Existing `SpendLedger` reserves a conservative per-run amount and refuses further model calls when its estimate exceeds the remaining reservation; actual provider overage can still occur, and the ledger is in process. The live request must set the authorized $1 budget and verify its reservation and provider-side backstop before execution. Previous Jacquemus 256-second/32-call/$0.0172 result is historical and not comparable as a single-hypothesis measurement. Time to first meaningful finding, total time, retries, model effort/tokens/spend and low-value calls require a new authorized live trace.

Ownership audit: KEEP `AgentRuntime`, skill registry, typed Qloo adapter, claim validator, baseline storage, recorded Atlas and comparison methodology. RESTRICT recorded canaries and synthetic worlds to scripts/evals/tests. CONSOLIDATE only the single-hypothesis result presentation inside the existing brand workspace. DELETE nothing in this sprint; static references and historical readers still use the older routes. Baseline PostgreSQL durability does not extend to in-process agent jobs. A restart loses job state and idempotency; uncertain dispatched calls are never resumed automatically. No schema, migration, secret, admission flag or production deployment changes are part of this work.

**Approval request, not execution:** one Loewe / user-proposed boutique hospitality investigation through `POST /api/agent/jobs` on an isolated local, continuously running backend. This route needs no baseline scan or database migration. Run with `NODE_ENV=development`, `CE_PUBLIC_DEPLOYMENT=0`, `CE_ACCEPT_INVESTIGATIONS=1`, `CE_BASELINE_ENABLED=0`, `CE_BASELINE_LIVE_ENABLED=0`, `CE_BASELINE_TOTAL_QLOO_CALLS=0`, `CE_SPEND_BUDGET_USD=1`, `CE_INVESTIGATION_RESERVE_USD=1`, and the already configured hackathon Qloo origin/credential and Anthropic credential. Unset `AGENT_MODEL` and `AGENT_EFFORT` overrides and verify the runtime reports `claude-haiku-5-5` at high effort before dispatch. These values apply **only** to that local process; keep production/public admission off. Use a provider-side Anthropic workspace cap as a durable backstop. Allow only `describe`, `find_tags`, `entity_tags` and bounded `recommend` through the existing registry/analysis collector, plus local deterministic tools. Use at most 12 Qloo dispatches including retries, a $1 Anthropic reservation, and at most four minutes. The spend guard refuses a next call when its conservative ceiling exceeds the reservation, but an in-flight call can slightly exceed its estimate; a strict $1 billed ceiling cannot be promised by the in-process ledger alone. Record actual model ID/effort, tokens, call IDs, status, retry counts, evidence-bearing vs low-value calls, spend and time to first meaningful result. Success requires a source-linked, claim-valid answer to the specific question with honest unknowns; an inconclusive or negative result can be useful. Stop on admission failure, contract drift, unresolved identity, unknown provider dispatch outcome, budget, timeout or source/claim validation failure. Do not replay uncertain calls after a restart.

Expected observations are a fresh Qloo brand resolution, concept search, bounded concept-member/brand-conditioned queries where supported, and the resulting source IDs or explicit empty/failed/needs-input statuses. No particular boutique-hospitality tag, member or positive verdict is assumed. The job response and complete trace are saved locally with secrets scrubbed; the local process is stopped after review. A missed or interrupted job is reported as such and is never retried automatically.

Sprint 9F can address measured usefulness gaps, explainability only after live contract verification, a controlled LLM-only benchmark and durable agent jobs only if the approved live evaluation demonstrates a need. No 9F implementation or production promotion is implied here.
